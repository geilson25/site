Insider Threat Detection POC

A Proof of Concept system for the proactive detection of insider-driven data exfiltration. It ingests telemetry from Wazuh, Suricata, and the ELK Stack, applies Process Mining to reconstruct behavioral event sequences, and uses Isolation Forest, PCA, and K-Means to flag statistically anomalous behavior in near real time.

An interinstitutional Software Factory initiative developing a mobile application focused on the wellbeing of healthcare professionals at Hospital Santa Joana.
