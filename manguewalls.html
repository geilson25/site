Community · Enthusiast Project

Manguewalls — cybersecurity, rooted in the mangue.

A WhatsApp community founded by Geilson Silva, bringing together people who study, work in, or are simply curious about cybersecurity — discussed with the informal, grounded spirit of Recife's mangue culture.

Overview

Technical depth, without losing the local voice.

Manguewalls started as a personal, enthusiast-run initiative: a space to talk shop about threat intelligence, defensive tooling, and the day-to-day of security work, without the stiffness of a formal forum. The name itself carries the idea — mangue, the mangrove ecosystem that shapes Recife's identity and culture, fused with walls, a nod to the defenses at the heart of cybersecurity.

The tone is deliberately informal. Members go by nicknames like "Crabmen" — a wink to the crab (caranguejo), a long-standing symbol of Recife and of the Manguebeat cultural movement — and technical discussion sits comfortably next to memes, book recommendations, and community banter.
