Current Research

Detecting the deviation before the breach.

Geilson's research investigates how insider threats — whether malicious, negligent, or the result of compromised credentials — can be identified through the behavioral traces they leave in system event logs, long before a full-scale data exfiltration event unfolds.

His work applies Process Mining to reconstruct how users actually behave, then compares that reality against expected patterns. The result is a Proof of Concept that pairs process discovery with unsupervised machine learning — Isolation Forest, PCA, and K-Means — layered on telemetry from Wazuh, Suricata, and the ELK Stack.

This research was formalized in "DT-Leak: Process Mining-Based Behavioral Detection of Data Exfiltration," accepted at SBSEG 2026, Brazil's leading conference in information and systems security.
